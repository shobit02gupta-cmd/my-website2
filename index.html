About vijaya

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO in the state which was formally registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 14th October 1996 bearing Registration No- “20154/118-1996-97”. The Registered office of the organization is located at 417, Sahid Nagar, Bhubaneswar. The organization was registered under Foreign Contribution (Registration Act) 1976 on 14th July, 2000 and also registered under U/S 80 G (5) & U/S 12 AA of Income Tax Act 1961. VIJAYA here under as stated has very clear vision, mission, goals and objectives.

Vision

VIJAYA aspires a just society, where people irrespective of cast & creed, rich & poor, powerful & powerless, male & female live at par with each other following the values of the sustainable development, global common hood and empathy towards each others.

Mission

Initiate people oriented development process in which men and women of poverty groups (Backward, Tribal and Costal) shall come together to change their powerlessness situation and enhance their socio economic health and educational standard and carry on the process even after the NGO withdraws.

Our objectives

The objectives of the organization are to create awareness among women and children on legal and constitutional rights, to give protection against injustice, atrocities and trafficking elimination of discrimination against girl child (Gender equity) and to create and strength self- reliance and self confidence. The organization aims to create scope of participation in development activities without any discretion of caste, creed or religion. It aims at empowering the youths of both sexes especially those belong to weaker section of society. It is committed to give institutional support to destitute, old, helpless and disadvantaged people and rehabilitate them in the society restoring their dignity.

Goals

  • Building capacity of the communities ensuring gender equity.
  • Institutional development of partners Organization learning from each other strength.
  • Provide consultancy services / support to the grass root organizations.
  • Policy advocacy reforms and networking.

Activities

We have a constant track record of notable: achievements in various fields/areas in past 15 years. A synoptive note on such activities is furnished below:-

  • Participation in various reliefs, rehabilitation, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) programme.
  • Operation of mobile medical health services.
  • Arranging of issue based workshops, seminars etc.
  • Undertaking ISD, Induction, Sensitization programmes like training of Medical Officers and Para-Medical personnel under RCH programmes.
  • Managing Special School of Blind, Deaf & Dumb Girls.
  • Managing SWADHAR Homes accommodating and taking care of distressed, disadvantaged and deserted widows and women.
  • Undertaking preparation of consumable items like curd, chhatua, wicks, badi and serving activities like supply of daily OMFED MILK through our distribution booth and grinding of wheat, chilly, turmeric and other spices.
  • Undertaking surveys, field studies.
  • Conducting awareness training for HIV/AIDS and nutrition and educational programmes for tribal population.
  • Undertaking Catering Services.
  • Undertaking translation of Trauma Counseling Modules developed by American Red Cross (New Delhi) and Asha Training Modules, SAB Training Modules etc into Oriya.
  • Undertaking programmes for nutrition and educational achievement for Chikutia Bhunjia Tribal people in Nuapada District.

our resource Strength

  • We have a panel of eminent and experienced Resource personnel belonging to various areas of specialization who are constantly with us in all our training & survey programme.
  • We have permanent infrastructure in the form of training Halls, Black & White Board, Over head Projects, TV, VCD etc and required furniture and fixtures.
  • We have a 10 seated vehicle with us with a permanent driver.
  • We have telephone and internet connection, fax and other accessories.
  • We have long experience and expertise in conducting various surveys and training programme, relief and rehabilitation programme.
  • We have wide networking with sisterly organizations all over the state and outside.

our project locations

Bhubaneswar

BHUBANESWAR OFFICE functions as the headquarter office and also the place where most of the training programmes are conducted. The office is well furnished & well equipped and is managed by a team of qualified and experienced staff as a front running resource centre.

puri

SWADHAR HOME AT PURI is a central Government funded project with an intake capacity of 150 helpless, distressed, deserted and deprived widows, women and girls who are not merely provided with fooding and accomodation, income generating programme and accommodation but also health care, counseling and vocational training services. A number of resource augmenting schemes like making of incense sticks, Wicks, Chhatua & Cereal, Phenyl etc are being implemented through the active participation of inmates of Swadhar.

A Special Residential Shool For Deaf, Dumb & Blind Girls.

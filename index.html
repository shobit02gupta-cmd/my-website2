WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Vijaya

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO in the state which was formally registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 14th October 1996 bearing Registration No- “20154/118-1996-97”. The Registered office of the organization is located at 417, Sahid Nagar, Bhubaneswar. The organization was registered under Foreign Contribution (Registration Act) 1976 on 14th July, 2000 and also registered under U/S 80 G (5) & U/S 12 AA of Income Tax Act 1961. VIJAYA here under as stated has very clear vision, mission, goals and objectives.

Our vision is to build a society where every individual gets equal opportunities to learn, grow and live with dignity.

OUR MISSION

Our Objectives

The objectives of the organization are to create awareness among women and children on legal and constitutional rights, to give protection against injustice, atrocities and trafficking elimination of discrimination against girl child (Gender equity) and to create and strength self- reliance and self confidence. The organization aims to create scope of participation in development activities without any discretion of caste, creed or religion. It aims at empowering the youths of both sexes especially those belong to weaker section of society. It is committed to give institutional support to destitute, old, helpless and disadvantaged people and rehabilitate them in the society restoring their dignity.

Our locations

Bhubaneswar

BHUBANESWAR OFFICE functions as the headquarter office and also the place where most of the training programmes are conducted. The office is well furnished & well equipped and is managed by a team of qualified and experienced staff as a front running resource centre.

puri

SWADHAR HOME AT PURI is a central Government funded project with an intake capacity of 150 helpless, distressed, deserted and deprived widows, women and girls who are not merely provided with fooding and accomodation, income generating programme and accommodation but also health care, counseling and vocational training services. A number of resource augmenting schemes like making of incense sticks, Wicks, Chhatua & Cereal, Phenyl etc are being implemented through the active participation of inmates of Swadhar.

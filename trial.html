Welcome to Vijaya

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO in the state which was formally
registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 14th
October 1996 bearing Registration No- “20154/118-1996-97”
. The Registered office of the organization is located at 417
, Sahid Nagar, Bhubaneswar. The organization has been
registered under Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act 1976
and also registered under U/S 80 G (5) & U/S 12 AA of Income
Tax Act 1961. VIJAYA as stated here has very clear vision
, mission, goals and objectives.

WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Vijaya

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO working for the welfare and development of people in Odisha. The organization works towards creating opportunities and improving the quality of life of people who need support.

Our vision is to build a society where every individual gets equal opportunities to learn, grow and live with dignity.
